Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Catalyzt mobile application and related services operated by MoonWorks ("Service Provider," "we," "us," or "our").

Catalyzt is designed with privacy as a core principle. Catalyzt does not require an account, and MoonWorks does not collect, transmit, or store your personal app data on external servers.

Your energy patterns, reflections, and personal experiments are yours. Information you choose to enter into Catalyzt remains stored locally on your device.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Information you choose to enter into Catalyzt is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to MoonWorks servers.

This includes:

  • Energy check-ins
  • Notes and reflections
  • Boosters and drainers
  • Catalyst selections
  • Experiment history
  • App preferences and settings
  • Other information you choose to add within the app

MoonWorks does not access, sell, or use your personal app data to create user profiles or for advertising purposes.

Device Backups: If you enable device backups through your operating system, such as Apple iCloud Backup or Android backup services, Catalyzt data may be included in those backups according to your device settings and the policies of those providers.

Information Collection and Use

MoonWorks does not actively collect personal information through its own servers.

Catalyzt may access limited device information necessary for app functionality, such as:

  • Mobile operating system information

Catalyzt does not collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, or behavioral data through its own servers.

Certain third-party services used by the app, such as subscription management, optional feedback collection, and platform-provided services, may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

Voice Logging

If you choose to use voice logging, your audio is temporarily sent to your device's built-in speech recognition service for transcription:

  • Apple Speech Recognition on iOS
  • Google Speech Recognition on Android

Audio processing occurs through these services and is not transmitted to or stored by MoonWorks.

The resulting text transcription is stored locally within Catalyzt on your device.

Apple and Google may process or retain voice data according to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

Catalyzt uses limited third-party services to provide certain features.

Subscription Management (RevenueCat and App Stores)

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

Catalyzt uses RevenueCat to verify subscription status and manage access to Pro features. RevenueCat may process limited purchase-related information, such as anonymous purchase identifiers or subscription status information.

Your personal app content, including energy logs, reflections, experiment history, and personal notes, is never shared with RevenueCat or payment platforms.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Optional Feedback and Updates (Tally)

If you choose to submit feedback or provide your email address to receive Catalyzt updates, your information will be processed through Tally.

Any email address you provide will only be used for Catalyzt-related communications, such as:

  • Launch announcements
  • Major feature updates
  • Responses to feedback you submit

Your email address will not be used for unrelated marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Retention, Access, and Deletion

Because your Catalyzt data is stored locally on your device, MoonWorks does not have remote access to your in-app data.

To delete your Catalyzt data, uninstall the application from your device. Your locally stored app data will be removed according to your device's storage behavior.

If you provide an email address through optional feedback or update forms, MoonWorks retains that information only as long as necessary for the purpose it was provided.

Your Privacy Rights (Including California CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information you provide directly to MoonWorks, including:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected
  • The right to request deletion of personal information provided directly to us
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

MoonWorks does not sell or share your personal app data.

To exercise applicable privacy rights regarding information provided directly to MoonWorks, contact:

moonworks.devlab@gmail.com

Security

Catalyzt stores your app data locally on your device.

Because your data is stored locally, the security of your information depends on the security protections of your device, including features such as your device passcode, biometric protections, and operating system security updates.

MoonWorks does not store your personal app data on external servers and does not maintain a database containing your private reflections, energy logs, or personal patterns.

Email addresses collected through optional feedback or update forms are handled securely and are accessible only to MoonWorks.

MoonWorks does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
